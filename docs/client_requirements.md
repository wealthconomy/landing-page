## Wealth Portfolios Sub-Pages Detailed Content
Explore diverse wealth growth options to meet your needs - simple, secure, and goal-driven.

### 1. WealthFlex
- **Hook**: Don’t be stranded... Flexible savings to meet your emergency needs
- **Key details**: Instant withdrawals, 7 free withdrawals / month, Interest/Impact accrues daily
- **Description**: Earn or Impact while keeping cash accessible for unexpected needs. Perfect for an emergency buffer without penalties.
- **CTA**: Start flexible saving

### 2. WealthFlow
- **Hook**: Consistency without stress
- **Key details**: Set a schedule (daily, weekly, monthly), Link a funding source, Automate transfers so your savings grow without thinking about it. Review progress, pause or adjust anytime.
- **Description**: Automate your savings and go to sleep while your wealth accumulates.
- **CTA**: Automate my savings

### 3. WealthGoal
- **Hook**: Save with discipline and smash your goals with targeted savings
- **Key details**: Create a goal (trip, tuition, down payment), choose a target amount and deadline, save manually or enable auto-debit to stay on track. Receive visual progress and reminders.
- **Description**: Keeps you motivated until you hit the goal.
- **CTA**: Create a goal

### 4. WealthFix
- **Hook**: Locked savings for guaranteed returns
- **Key details**: Lock in your savings for a fixed term, Choose the term length that matches your plan, No withdrawals until maturity, Reward for discipline and penalty for default.
- **Description**: Keeps you disciplined and rewarded. Stay disciplined and fix your money or savings for guaranteed returns.
- **CTA**: Lock and grow

### 5. WealthFam
- **Hook**: Build financial security for those you love
- **Key details**: Create plans for spouses, children, or relatives, Manage contributions, Set individual goals.
- **CTA**: Start a family plan

### 6. WealthGroup
- **Hook**: Collective saving made easy
- **Key details**: Create or join a group with friends, colleagues or community members, Set contribution rules, timelines and shared goals, Track group progress.
- **CTA**: Start a Group, Join a Group

- **Recommendation prompt**: Need help choosing the right portfolio? We’ll recommend the best fit based on your goals in the app.
- **CTA**: Get Started
